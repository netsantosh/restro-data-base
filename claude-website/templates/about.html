What {{ site.site_name }} is for

Choosing a restaurant usually means jumping between maps, review apps and photo galleries. {{ site.site_name }} puts the useful parts on one page: the cuisine, the rating, a few photos, the address, the phone number and a link to directions. The guide is organised the way people actually decide, first the city, then the neighbourhood, then the place.

Where the information comes from

Names, addresses, phone numbers, ratings, review counts and photos are collected from public Google Maps listings. Ratings and review counts are Google Maps figures. They are not reviews written on this site, and we do not write or sell reviews.

Photos are checked automatically before they are shown, and profile pictures and images that do not show food or a restaurant are removed. The check is not perfect, so if you see a photo that does not belong, please tell us.

How restaurants are ranked

Lists start with the best rated places. The order uses the Google Maps rating together with the number of reviews, so a restaurant with a 4.8 rating from thousands of diners ranks above one with a 5.0 from only a few. Use the filters on area pages to narrow the list by cuisine or rating.

Keeping details accurate

Restaurants open, close, move and change their numbers. The data is refreshed regularly, but some details can be out of date. Always confirm opening hours with the restaurant before you travel.

If you spot a mistake, a closed restaurant or a wrong photo, or if you own a restaurant and want details corrected or a listing removed, use the contact page and include the address of the page. We will review every request.
