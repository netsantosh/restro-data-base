About {{ site.site_name }}

{{ site.site_name }} is a restaurant directory. It lists restaurants by country, state, city and area so you can compare places to eat in one place.

Where the data comes from

Names, addresses, phone numbers, ratings and photos are collected from public Google Maps listings. Ratings and review counts shown here are Google Maps figures and are refreshed when the listings are updated. They are not reviews written on this site.

Corrections

If a restaurant has closed, moved or has wrong details, please contact us with the page address and we will correct it.
