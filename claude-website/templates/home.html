    About {{ site.site_name }}

    {{ site.site_name }} is a restaurant directory built around how people actually choose where to eat: first the destination, then the neighbourhood, then the place. Each area page lists the restaurants there with their cuisine, rating, address and contact details, so you can compare quickly.

    Ratings and review counts shown on this site are taken from public Google Maps listings and are refreshed when the listings are updated. They are not reviews written on this site. If a place has closed or moved, let us know and we will fix it.
